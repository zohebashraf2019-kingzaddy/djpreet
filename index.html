Meet the DJ

The man behind the booth

DJ Preet (Manny Bhambra, @djpreet775) is a live event mobile DJ based in the Reno and Carson City area. He brings high-energy, open-format sets built on the music the desi community grew up dancing to, from Punjabi bhangra and Bollywood chartbusters to South Indian bangers and old-school Hindi classics, blended with the hip-hop and Top 40 your other guests know by heart.

He reads a room the way a host does. The aunties get their classics, the cousins get their bhangra drops, and the dance floor stays full from the first song to the last. You'll find him behind the decks at mehndi nights, sangeet stages, Vaisakhi celebrations at the Grand Sierra, three-deck cruise parties and Desi Saturdays at The Wolf Den.

      FAQ

      Good to know

      How far in advance should I book?

      Wedding season and festival dates (Vaisakhi, Diwali, New Year's) fill up fast. Six to twelve months ahead is ideal for weddings, and four to eight weeks usually works for parties.

      Do you travel outside Reno?

      Yes. Reno, Sparks, Carson City and Lake Tahoe are home turf. Travel to Sacramento, the Bay Area and beyond is available, and a travel fee may apply.

      Can we send song requests and a do-not-play list?

      Absolutely. You'll go over must-plays, first dance, entrances, performance tracks and a do-not-play list during your planning call.

      Do you bring your own sound and lights?

      Yes. Pro speakers, wireless mics and dance-floor lighting come with private event packages. For club nights he can plug into the house system.

      How does the deposit work?

      A 30% deposit holds your date and the balance is due the week of the event. Online deposit payments are coming to this site soon.
